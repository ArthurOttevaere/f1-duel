        Championship

        Points race by race — follow the title fight and position swaps.

        Title race

        Who can still mathematically win the drivers' championship.

        A driver is out once their best possible total (winning every remaining race & sprint) can no longer reach the current leader's points. Remaining points = races × 25 + sprints × 8.

        Factors come from the model (XGBoost · SHAP values). Boost = pushes forward, penalty = holds back.

        Session data

        Practice and qualifying standings used as model input.

        If fewer than 20 drivers appear, FastF1 may have incomplete session data cached — restart the server and try again after the session is fully uploaded.
